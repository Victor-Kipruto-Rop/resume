Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading, legal compliance, and account tiers.

Is TuneFlow completely free to use?
Yes! TuneFlow offers a robust free tier with lightning-fast standard downloads, album art embedding, and access to all supported platforms. You can optionally upgrade to Pro for lossless FLAC and priority servers.
What audio formats and bitrates are supported?
We support MP3 at 128kbps, 192kbps, and 320kbps, as well as lossless FLAC, WAV, and AAC formats depending on the original source availability.
Can I download full playlists and albums at once?
Absolutely. Simply paste any public Spotify, YouTube Music, or SoundCloud playlist link into the search or download bar, and TuneFlow will package all tracks into a clean ZIP archive.
Is my personal data and download history secure?
We adhere to a strict privacy-first policy. We do not track, log, or store any links you download or personal query data on our servers.
