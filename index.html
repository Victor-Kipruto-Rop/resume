Warehousing & Architecture

Data Quality & Reliability

  • Idempotent Upserts: Ensuring jobs can re-run at any point without producing duplicate rows.
  • Deduplication Logic: Aggregating quantities on conflicting records rather than blindly dropping them.
  • Validation Engines: Building multi-stage cleaning pipelines (null handling, type coercion) via config-driven rules.
  • Pytest: Comprehensive unit & integration testing (null injection, coercion failures).

Visualisation
